## Agent Brief

**Summary:** Let authorized staff download a clearly identified Odoo-generated PDF for a verified Revolut payment or refund, immediately or later, from both Odoo interfaces.

**Current behavior:** The integration records verified financial outcomes and includes Revolut references in checkout receipt information. It does not provide the agreed historical payment/refund PDF experience. Older operation records may not contain optional card-display metadata or the original provider transaction timestamp; verification time is not automatically the provider's payment time.

**Desired behavior:** Staff find the relevant Odoo sale, select its verified Revolut payment or refund, and download the corresponding PDF without charging, refunding, settling or reopening the transaction. Documents remain available after reload, session closure and later refunds. The PDF can be printed or manually emailed outside the module. No accounting invoice or customer record is required solely to obtain a payment receipt.

**Key interfaces:**

- Verified payment and refund operations supply authoritative eligibility, amounts, currency, original/refund links and references; receipt code does not create a second financial-state authority.
- Existing POS order history and administration order/payment navigation expose the same server-authorized document behavior.
- Odoo reporting and download facilities render the PDF using normal project conventions. Reuse existing mechanisms where they satisfy this contract.
- Server-side access checks cover both document entry points and any stored report attachments; browser state is never proof of payment or permission.

- [x] Each verified full or partial refund has a distinct downloadable refund PDF from both interfaces, showing its verified refund amount/currency, refund reference and original-payment relationship. Multiple partial refunds remain individually identifiable. Original payment receipts remain available after partial or full refund; the original payment and later refund documents do not overwrite each other or misrepresent each other's dates and amounts.
- [x] Receipt eligibility is enforced server-side from authoritative verified outcomes. Unverified, pending, failed, declined, cancelled or uncertain attempts cannot produce a successful-payment or completed-refund receipt through UI actions, direct requests or attachment access. A verified partial tender may have its own payment receipt, but it must not claim the whole POS sale is fully paid. Selecting a payment in a split-tender sale must not substitute the order total or include another tender's amount.

Downloading an already available receipt must not depend on terminal availability or a live provider response. Missing/inconsistent required evidence or PDF generation failures produce an actionable message without changing the payment/refund outcome.
- [x] Deliver downloadable PDFs suitable for ordinary printing. Staff handle email manually. A physical-printer failure or cancellation must not change transaction state. No automatic email, public QR receipt sharing, direct TPE printer command, or accounting invoice/credit-note creation is introduced by this slice.
