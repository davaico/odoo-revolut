1. **Saved-refund checkout recovery fails after injected checkout rejection and
   timeout.** Both
   `TestRevolutVerifiedCheckout.test_verified_saved_refund_checkout_rejection_recovers_without_second_refund`
   and
   `TestRevolutVerifiedCheckout.test_verified_saved_refund_checkout_timeout_recovers_without_second_refund`
   fail at tour step **35/42**, “Double Check status remains one bounded read-only
   restoration attempt.” A “Revolut checkout recovery” modal covers Check status;
   the tour cannot act beneath it and times out at 10,000 ms. Both fail in the
   complete suite and in the fresh focused run on the identical frozen image.
   Neither container was OOM-killed. Determine and correct the recovery/dialog
   behavior or demonstrated tour assumption while preserving payment guards.
   Prove successful completion of the same return with original refund/tender
   references and no duplicate financial POST, then rerun the affected pair and
   complete suite on a newly frozen candidate. The final provider-count and
   completed-return assertions in these failed tours were not reached.
2. **Optional `pos_hr` permission test errors during setup.**
   `TestSharedPosSessionAccess.test_employee_list_does_not_restrict_backend_user_rpc`
   writes `module_pos_hr=True` on an already-open register. Native Odoo raises
   “Unable to modify this PoS Configuration because you can't modify Module Pos Hr
   while a session is open.” Correct the fixture's native configuration/session
   ordering and execute its intended employee-selection/backend-permission
   assertions. The standard lane's optional skip does not resolve this error.

No candidate source, assertions or timeout limits were changed during validation.
