How it works

From checkout to confirmation.

01

Send from the register

Choose Revolut on the payment screen. The terminal receives the amount for that payment line.

02

Pay on the terminal

The customer follows the terminal prompts and can choose a tip when the terminal is configured for tipping.

03

Verify, then complete

Odoo verifies the payment, base amount and tip. A fully paid order completes when native checkout requirements are met.

Features

More than sending an amount.

Registers

Retail & Restaurant

Use retail registers or Restaurant table and Takeout flows. Restaurant fulfilment follows the table and order preset.

Checkout

Split payments & terminal tips

Combine cash and Revolut, or charge a partial card amount. Tips chosen on the terminal are verified and reflected in Odoo.

Refunds

Full & partial refunds

Authorized managers refund against the original payment, with separate base and tip allocations and server-enforced quantity and amount limits.

Recovery

Recovery & reconciliation

Check the original payment after an interruption. Scheduled reconciliation records late outcomes; managers review unresolved attempts.

External refunds

Refunds made outside Odoo

Discover refunds issued through Revolut, then complete or link the matching native return or accounting correction, without sending another refund.

Security

Server-side credentials

Merchant secrets remain in the server environment. Company authority and register access are controlled in the backend.

Payment safety

A timeout does not prove failure.

Keep the original order and payment line, reconnect and check the status before retrying. A success on the terminal alone never completes an Odoo sale.

Check before retrying

Recover the original payment after an interruption instead of charging twice.

Verify before completing

Payment, base amount and tip are checked before native Odoo validation.

Reconcile late outcomes

Scheduled actions record payments and refunds that settle after the fact.

Setup

Configuration, in four stages.

01

Install the module

Place pos_revolut on your server's addon path, update the Apps list and install POS Revolut. Its Point of Sale and Restaurant dependencies install automatically.

02

Authorize your Merchant credentials

Store the secret in a server environment variable beginning with ODOO_REVOLUT_SECRET_. As administrator, authorize its reference for the company and environment under Revolut Merchant Authorities.

03

Connect the payment method

Select the Revolut terminal integration and set the environment, location, terminal and authorized secret reference. Use Check Connection, add the method to the register and enable automatic validation of terminal payments.

04

Finish register setup

Configure a suitable Tip Product, grant backend users register access, disable Restaurant Add tips after payment and enable the reconciliation scheduled actions. Reopen the POS to load its assets.

Check Connection discovers the terminal; it does not send a payment or verify every Merchant permission. Keep the installation, user and upgrade guides with your deployment.

Good to know

Current scope.

Internet access is required by the Odoo server and the terminal.

Tips are selected on the terminal. Odoo-entered tips and Restaurant Add tips after payment are unsupported. A correctly configured Tip Product is required even when tips are disabled.

Fulfilment: retail currently maps to Eat In. Restaurant supports table-based Eat In and recognized Takeout presets; delivery and unknown no-table presets are guarded.

Timed Takeout requires an available pickup date and time before collection.

Refunds issued through Revolut do not automatically return stock or credit an invoice. An authorized manager completes the native Odoo work.

External service & data

What is exchanged with Revolut?

The Odoo server makes authenticated requests to the Revolut Merchant API for terminal discovery, payments, refunds, status checks and payment reports. These requests use your Merchant credentials.

Terminal discovery & payments

Amount, currency, location and terminal identifiers, fulfilment type, POS register name, technical order and payment-line identifiers.

Status, recovery & refunds

Provider transaction references, refund amount and currency, technical refund and idempotency references, and lookup filters.

Payment verification

Payment-report type, time filters, timezone and requested report columns.

Not sent by this addon

Card details are handled by the terminal and Revolut. This addon does not send customer contact details or product lines.

Recorded in Odoo

Payment and refund references, verified amounts and statuses, to support reconciliation and recovery.
